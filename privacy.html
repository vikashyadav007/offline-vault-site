Offline Vault

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Offline Vault ("the app") is designed to work completely offline.

We collect nothing. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. It does not connect to the internet. On Android it does not request the internet permission at all.

What stays on your device

  • Photos of the cards and documents you add, and the details you type or the app recognises (for example card number, name and expiry date).
  • A salted hash of your PIN, stored in the iOS Keychain / Android Keystore.
  • Your Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint is handled entirely by your device's operating system. The app never sees biometric data.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera and photo picker only when you choose to add a document. Text recognition and auto-cropping run on your device.

Backups

If you tap "Export backup", the app creates a zip file and opens the system share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice. The app does not upload it anywhere. Vault data is excluded from automatic iCloud / Google cloud backups.

Deleting your data

Delete items or folders in the app, or uninstall the app, and all of its data is removed from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
