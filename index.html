Offline Vault

Your IDs and cards, never online.

Offline Vault keeps photos of your ID cards, credit and debit cards, licences, insurance cards and other important documents in one private place: your phone.

100% offline, by design

The app does not connect to the internet. There is no account, no cloud sync, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Text recognition and auto-crop run on your device.

Locked down

A 4-digit PIN with optional Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock. Vault data is excluded from cloud backups.

Available for iPhone, iPad and Android.
